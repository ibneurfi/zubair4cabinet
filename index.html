The Manifesto

Students' Charter for Systemic Reform

"सिर्फ हंगामा खड़ा करना मेरा मक़सद नहीं,
मेरा मक़सद लोगों की ज़रूरतों को समझना है।"

"Be the change that you wish to see in the world." — M.K. Gandhi

My dear fellow Aligarians, I stand before you not merely as a candidate, but as a student who has lived every facet of this great institution. My mission is to turn concerns into action and promises into measurable results.

1. Administrative Convenience & Helpdesk

  • Controller Office Navigability: A clear map and a dedicated Helpdesk at the Controller Office to prevent students from running table-to-table.
  • Active Helpline: Accessible, manned helpline numbers during working hours for immediate administrative support.
  • On-Site Photocopy Facility: A photocopy/Xerox provision directly at the Controller Office for emergency document needs.
  • Departmental Access: Bonafide students get access to departmental photocopy machines during working hours.

2. Academic Excellence & Infrastructure

  • Timely Convocation & Medals: Resolve pending Convocations and medal distributions stalled since 2018, ensuring punctual annual ceremonies.
  • Campus Wi-Fi Overhaul: Upgrade Wi-Fi infrastructure for seamless mobile accessibility, eliminating reliance on personal hotspots.
  • Laboratory Rejuvenation: Construct vital lab spaces (e.g., Geology, Education) and upgrade non-functional labs in the Faculty of Science.
  • Library Inclusivity & Orientation: Abolish restrictions on first-year students' access to Seminar Libraries, and run user-awareness programs for new students.

3. Women's Welfare, Safety & Empowerment

  • Accessible Campus Transit: Subsidized, secure e-rickshaw and shuttle services connecting Girls' Hostels to departments and inter-faculty transit points.
  • Dignity at Maulana Azad Library: Immediate deployment of female staff for the frisking and bag-checking of female students at the library exit.
  • Upgradation of Girls' Common Rooms (GCR): Hygienic, well-equipped Girls' Common Rooms across all faculties and departments.

4. Campus Life, Hygiene & Accountability

  • Standardized Canteen Rates: A universal, affordable rate list strictly enforced across all university canteens to prevent arbitrary pricing.
  • Campus Safety & Environment: Adequate street lighting and a humane, effective stray dog management protocol.
  • Student Union Accountability: A 'Public Promise Tracker', continuous grievance follow-up, monthly open houses, and an AMU Student Parliament to keep the university accountable.
